One system for warehouse, production, sales, staff and learning.

FlowCore is all-in-one business software built in Uzbekistan. Companies pick only the modules they need: warehouse stock, production orders, customers and sales, attendance and payroll, training centres, a shop till and finance. AI analytics and MCP connect your reports to AI assistants.

FlowCore modules

FlowCore ERP

FlowCore ERP tracks production orders, raw materials, bills of materials, work stages and finished goods. Production software from Uzbekistan for factories and sewing workshops.

FlowCore CRM

FlowCore CRM tracks leads, deals, follow-up calls and tasks, and receives Telegram and Instagram messages in one inbox. CRM software from Uzbekistan.

FlowCore WMS

FlowCore WMS records goods receipts, issues, transfers and stock levels across several warehouses, with stock value and low-stock alerts. Inventory software from Uzbekistan.

FlowCore HRM

FlowCore HRM records attendance with a Face ID tablet app and calculates payroll: fixed, hourly, daily, piece-rate, sales commission, bonuses and deductions.

FlowCore LMS

FlowCore LMS manages students, groups, timetables, attendance, grades and tuition payments, with a read-only portal for students and parents.

FlowCore POS

FlowCore POS handles shop sales with barcodes, payments, customer credit (nasiya), returns and cashier shifts, connected to warehouse stock.

FlowCore Finance

FlowCore Finance keeps cash, card and bank accounts in UZS and USD, with income and expense categories, transfers and currency exchange. Included as a bonus module.

FlowCore AI

FlowCore AI explains warehouse, production, CRM, HR and LMS reports. Connect your own OpenAI, Claude or Gemini key, or connect an AI agent to the FlowCore MCP server.

Why companies choose FlowCore

Pick only what you need

Start with one or two modules, such as warehouse and payroll, and add others later. Finance comes as a bonus with every plan that includes a module.

Cloud or your own computer

Work online at your company address <company>.flowcore.uz, or install the desktop edition on Windows or macOS and keep the data on that computer.

Roles and permissions

The owner decides which modules each employee can see or edit. Every company’s data is kept separate.

Mobile apps

FlowCore Rahbar (Android) shows reports and notifications to owners. FlowCore FaceID turns an Android tablet into a face-recognition attendance terminal.

Telegram and Instagram

CRM receives messages from Telegram and Instagram. A Telegram bot sends owners daily reports.

AI analytics and MCP

Each module has an AI analyst that works with your own AI key. AI agents can connect to your company’s FlowCore MCP server with read or write keys.

Frequently asked questions

What is FlowCore?

FlowCore is business management software from Uzbekistan. It covers warehouse stock (WMS), production (ERP), customers and sales (CRM), staff attendance and payroll (HRM), training centres (LMS), a shop till (POS), finance and AI analytics. The official website is flowcore.uz.

Which languages does FlowCore support?

The application interface is in Uzbek. This page describes the product in English; contact us for a walkthrough.

Can FlowCore run without the cloud?

Yes. Besides the online version there is a licensed desktop edition for Windows and macOS. Its data is stored on that computer; Telegram backups are optional.

How is the price calculated?

It depends on the modules, the number of users and whether you use the cloud or the desktop edition. Send a request and we will give you a quote.
