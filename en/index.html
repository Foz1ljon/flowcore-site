Frequently asked questions.

What is FlowCore and who is it for?

FlowCore brings inventory, manufacturing, customers, payroll, education, checkout and finance into one system. Businesses can start with the modules they need.

How does Face ID attendance work?

A compatible Android tablet records arrivals and departures using face recognition. The HR module shows attendance, hours worked and payroll.

Can I choose only the modules I need?

Yes. Start with the modules you use and add others later. Manufacturing works together with inventory.

What is the difference between browser and desktop use?

The browser version lets your team share records from different locations. The desktop version keeps records on your computer. Activation and Telegram or Instagram messaging require internet access.

Can staff see only the modules they need?

The owner or administrator sets what each employee can view or edit.

How is my company’s data kept separate?

Access is checked against the company and the user’s permissions. With the desktop version, records stay on your computer.

Can we start with our existing Excel records?

During the demo, we can review your spreadsheets and opening balances. The way to bring them in depends on their structure and size.

How do I connect Telegram and Instagram?

Connect each channel separately with the required account permissions. Telegram uses a bot token. Instagram requires a compatible professional account.

How do I request a demo?

Leave your name and phone number in the contact form. We’ll get in touch and show the modules that fit your workflow.

Is the name FlowCore, Flow Core or CoreFlow?

The official name is FlowCore and the official website is flowcore.uz.
