Frequently asked questions.

Can I choose only the modules I need?

Yes. Start with the modules you use and add others later. Manufacturing works together with inventory.

Can we start with our existing Excel records?

During the demo, we can review your spreadsheets and opening balances. The way to bring them in depends on their structure and size.

How do I connect Telegram and Instagram?

Connect each channel separately with the required account permissions. Telegram uses a bot token. Instagram requires a compatible professional account.
