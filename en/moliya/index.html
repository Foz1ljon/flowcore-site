FlowCore Moliya · Finance

Finance: accounts, income, expenses and transfers

FlowCore Finance shows how much money is in each account and where it went. Cash, card and bank accounts keep their own currency and balance.

Book a free demoTry the live demo (Uzbek)

What it does

Separate accounts

Open cash, card and bank accounts with their own currency and opening balance.

Income and expenses

Record money movements with date, account and category.

Transfers and exchange

Move money between accounts; for currency exchange the sent and received amounts are kept separately.

Exact money

Amounts are stored as exact decimals, never rounded floating-point numbers.

Linked payments

Optionally link POS, LMS and payroll payments to the account you choose.

Period report

Balances, income and expenses by account and category for any period.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to buy Finance separately?

No. Finance is a bonus included with every plan that has at least one module and with every desktop licence.

How is it different from POS?

POS runs sales, receipts, credit and cashier shifts. Finance tracks accounts, income, expenses, transfers and currency balances.
