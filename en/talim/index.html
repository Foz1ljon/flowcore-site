FlowCore LMS · Learning Management System

Software for training centres and schools

FlowCore LMS keeps a training centre or school in one system: courses and groups, lesson schedule, attendance, grades and payments. Students and parents see their own information in a portal.

Book a free demoTry the live demo (Uzbek)

What it does

Courses and groups

Create courses, open groups and assign teachers and students.

Timetable and attendance

Plan lessons and mark attendance for each group.

Grades

Record grades and follow each student’s progress.

Tuition payments and debts

Track payments and see who owes how much today.

Student and parent portal

Students and parents get read-only accounts to see schedule, attendance, grades and payments.

Reports

Active students, groups, revenue and debts for any period.

Frequently asked questions

Do portal accounts count toward the user limit?

No. Student and parent portal accounts are kept separately from staff users.

Can payments go to Finance?

Yes. Tuition payments can be linked to a FlowCore Finance account.

Is it only for training centres?

It also suits schools and other organisations that run groups and lessons.
