FlowCore HRM · Human Resource Management

HR software with Face ID attendance and payroll

FlowCore HRM connects attendance and payroll. Staff check in and out on an Android tablet with face recognition, and the month’s pay is calculated from the hours, days and work they actually did.

What it does

Face ID attendance terminal

The FlowCore FaceID app turns an Android tablet into a face-recognition terminal with liveness checks and an offline queue.

Mixed pay models

Combine fixed salary, hourly, daily shift, piece-rate, sales percentage, bonuses and deductions per employee.

Shifts and departments

Set up departments, positions and shifts that match your company.

Advances and payments

Record advances and payouts; they can be linked to FlowCore Finance accounts.

Piece-rate from production

Work logged in ERP stages flows into piece-rate pay.

Payroll reports

Days, hours, earnings and deductions for each employee, exported to Excel or PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Which device does Face ID need?

An Android tablet with a front camera. We check compatibility with you during onboarding.

Are face photos stored from each check-in?

No. Check-in snapshots are not sent or stored; only the attendance event is recorded.

Does it work with the desktop edition?

Yes. The licensed desktop edition receives tablet check-ins through an end-to-end encrypted relay.
