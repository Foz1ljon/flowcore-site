FlowCore CRM · Customer Relationship Management

CRM for customers, deals and messages

FlowCore CRM keeps every customer, deal and conversation in one workspace. Managers see the sales pipeline, the next step for each lead and messages from Telegram and Instagram.

Book a free demoTry the live demo (Uzbek)

What it does

Sales pipeline

Move deals through stages on a board and see the value of open and won deals.

Follow-ups and tasks

Plan calls, meetings, tasks and notes for each lead; the next step is shown on the card.

Telegram and Instagram inbox

Connect a Telegram bot, a Telegram profile and an Instagram professional account to answer customers in one place.

Regions and sources

Record the customer’s region of Uzbekistan and see revenue by region on a map.

Reports

Leads, won and lost deals, conversion and average deal size for any period, exported to Excel or PDF.

AI analyst

Ask the CRM AI analyst about the pipeline; customer names are never sent to the AI.

Frequently asked questions

Which channels can CRM connect?

Telegram bots, a Telegram profile (with your own API ID and hash) and Instagram professional accounts.

Can managers see only their own leads?

The owner sets which modules and actions each employee may use.

Does CRM work with the shop till?

POS customers and sales can be used alongside CRM; both are FlowCore modules.
