FlowCore ERP · Enterprise Resource Planning

ERP software for production and garment workshops

FlowCore ERP keeps production in one place: what an order is made of, who works on it and which stage it is at. A sewing workshop or any other factory sets up its own stages.

Book a free demoTry the live demo (Uzbek)

What it does

Record production orders

Enter the product, quantity and work stages such as cutting, sewing, ironing and packing, and see which orders are finished.

Know if raw materials are enough

Set how much fabric, buttons or other materials one product needs and check stock before you start.

Assign work to staff

Give each stage an employee, planned hours and instructions, and log completed work. Works together with HR piece-rate payroll.

Account for finished goods

When an order is completed, used materials leave the warehouse and finished goods are received. Defects and their reasons are recorded too.

Bill of materials

Keep material norms for every product and compare the required amount with warehouse stock.

Manager and worker in one system

The manager sees the order status, the worker sees the assigned stage, deadline and instructions.

Frequently asked questions

Does ERP need the warehouse module?

Yes. Production works together with FlowCore WMS so materials and finished goods stay in step with stock.

Is it suitable for a sewing workshop?

Yes. Stages, material norms and piece-rate work fit garment and textile production; other factories set their own stages.

Can I see defects?

Defective products and the reason are recorded and appear in production reports.
