FlowCore WMS · Warehouse Management System

Warehouse software for stock, receipts and issues

FlowCore WMS shows what is in stock, where it is and what it is worth. Receipts, issues and transfers between warehouses are recorded with a full history.

What it does

Several warehouses

Keep separate stock for every warehouse and see where each product is.

Receipts and issues

Record incoming and outgoing goods right from the product list, on a computer or a phone.

Movement history

Every receipt, issue and transfer is kept with date, quantity and the employee.

Low stock

See which products are running low before they run out.

Recipes and costing

Define product recipes and material consumption for production and cost price.

Reports and exports

Stock value, movements and balances for any period, exported to Excel or PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Can I keep stock in several warehouses?

Yes. Each warehouse has its own balance and goods can be transferred between them.

Can I start from Excel?

We review your spreadsheet structure and opening balances together during onboarding.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. On phones the product list shows cards with large receipt and issue buttons.
